Introduction When factors such as custom, habit, sharia, unwritten social rules and giving importance to the views and judgments of others in the society cause the separation of generations and this problem progresses to such an extent that it even affects interpersonal relationships in middle age and old age and makes people one-dimensional, isolated and depressed, perhaps it is necessary to solve this problem through cultural changes. In the fiction book "Let's fall in love until we die", it has been tried to deal with this issue in a gentle and reasonable manner, in accordance with social changes, and in a way, unspoken words and hidden grudges have been brought up. In this regard, in addition to subjecting middle-aged men and women who sit in parks and struggle with loneliness at home, efforts have been made to question and negate some taboos or social contracts that disturb the security and comfort of this group of people in the country, and to introduce healthy and alternative relationships. We hope that everyone, with a positive view of mothers and fathers who are left alone or have emotional problems, will help us in understanding and conveying value concepts such as honoring parents and paying attention to the needs of middle-aged or elderly loved ones. On the other hand, we hope that changing the approach of the stories from emotional-emotional to serious and combative, can help to correctly explain the position of the generation that is left behind from the enthusiasm of life.
